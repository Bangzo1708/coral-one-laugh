A single original one-liner, capped at 200 characters, written to land its laugh on a first read. Three candidate lines were drafted and scored against the brief (surprise turn, economy, freshness); the strongest — a barista asked for something strong who hands over a resignation letter instead — is written to entry.txt.

```sh
node main.ts
```
